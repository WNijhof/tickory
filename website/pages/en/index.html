The to-do list you host yourself

Tickory is a to-do app for you, your family or a small team, running on your own server. Your data stays at home, the app keeps working without a connection, and it reminds you when a task is due.

Try the demo Install it yourself

Free software (AGPL-3.0) · one Docker container · no account with anyone else · the app in English or Dutch

Why Tickory

  • Self-hosted

    One small Docker container on a mini PC, NAS or Raspberry Pi. No npm packages and no build step: there is little that can break.

  • Your data at home

    Everything lives in a single file on your own server. Nobody else reads along, and a backup is one command.

  • No subscription

    Tickory is free software. No monthly fee and no features behind a paywall.

  • Works offline

    The app keeps your tasks on your device. Without a connection you simply carry on; everything syncs by itself afterwards.

  • Together

    Everyone gets their own account and their own Inbox. Share a project with your housemates or colleagues and assign tasks to each other.

What you do with it

All screenshots below come from the app itself, in English, with the sample data of the demo. The sample household is Dutch, so its names are too.

Type it the way you say it

One line is enough. Loodgieter bellen morgen om 9 uur /Klusjes @bellen p1 !1u (“call the plumber tomorrow at 9”) becomes a task for tomorrow 9:00, in the section Klusjes, with the label bellen, the highest priority and a reminder an hour before. Below the field you see right away what was recognised.

The app is available in English and Dutch, but the dates and recurrences you type are recognised in Dutch for now: over twee weken (in two weeks), voor Sinterklaas (before 5 December), elke tweede dinsdag (every second Tuesday). Dates like 31-12, 31-12-2026 and 2026-12-31T14:30 work in any language.

All dates and recurrences

Today and Upcoming

shows what is due today and what is overdue, from all your projects together. shows the next two weeks, day by day.

A recurring task you tick off moves on to its next date. Ticking off can be undone with one click.

More about the views

Together in one project

Share a project by typing the username of a housemate or colleague. Everyone sees and edits the same tasks and sections, and someone else's changes show up within a few seconds.

Assign a task to whoever is going to do it. Your Inbox and your labels remain yours alone.

Sharing and working together

Everything about a task in one place

Click a task for its details: description, due date with quick choices, deadline, priority, labels, project and section, reminders, and subtasks. At the bottom are the comments: talk with the others in a shared project, or make a note for yourself. In the date field you type the same as when adding a task.

It works with the keyboard too: q adds a task and Ctrl+Enter saves.

You drag tasks and projects to their place, with the mouse or your finger, or with Alt and the arrow keys. How moving works.

Your projects, kept in order

Give every project its own color, put your favorites at the top of the sidebar and hang projects under each other as subprojects. When a project is finished you archive it: it disappears from view and you can bring it back later.

Projects and sections · The screen

Reminders that go off

A task with a time gets a reminder by itself, 30 minutes before by default. You can add your own in the task window or in the line itself: Tandarts bellen morgen om 9 uur !30m.

The notification arrives in your browser (over HTTPS) or in the Android app, with buttons to tick the task off or postpone it ten minutes. Reminders are personal, also in a shared project.

All about reminders

On your phone, even without signal

Tickory is a web app that you install as an app on your phone and computer. Without a connection it still opens and you can add and tick off tasks; as soon as the connection is back, everything goes to the server.

For that, your server has to be reachable over HTTPS. For Android there will also be an app of its own (APK) that sets your reminders as alarms on the phone, without Google services; the first version comes with the next release.

Install in three steps

You need a computer that stays on, with Docker and docker compose. The image is available for ordinary PCs and NAS systems (amd64) and for a Raspberry Pi with a 64-bit system (arm64).

  1. Get the files

    git clone {{repo}}.git
cd tickory
cp .env.example .env

  2. Choose the first account

    Put a username and a password of at least 10 characters in .env. This account is created on the first start and is an administrator: you use it to create the other accounts in the app.

    TODO_ADMIN_USER=anna
TODO_ADMIN_PASSWORD=choose-a-long-password-here

  3. Start

    docker compose up -d
docker compose ps          # after a few seconds: (healthy)

    Then open http://127.0.0.1:8787 on the server itself and sign in.

This is what you start with that: the docker-compose.yml from the repository, shown here without its (Dutch) comments.

Set up like this, the container is only reachable from the server itself. For your phone and other devices you put HTTPS in front of it, which is possible without exposing anything to the internet. The guide explains how: installing, HTTPS, backup and updating.

What it cannot do (yet)

Tickory is young. The core works and is tested, but much of what you know from the big to-do apps is still missing. Better to be honest up front than to disappoint you later.

Coming

  • The Android app (APK). Built and tested on a computer, but there is no released APK yet: the first one comes with the next version, and the maker has not yet tried it on a real phone. Until then you use the web app in your phone's browser. Current state
  • Filters and search. The filter language and the search are built, but there are no screens for them yet, so you cannot use them yet.
  • Date language in English. The app itself is bilingual (English and Dutch), but what you type (morgen om 9 uur, elke maandag) is recognised in Dutch only for now.

On the server, not yet in the app

  • The duration of a task, and a board or calendar view

Missing entirely

  • Your own order for Today and Upcoming, and an order of their own for favourites
  • Managing labels: renaming, deleting, filtering by label
  • Attachments, import and export, an iCal feed, widgets, statistics, templates
  • Inviting by link or e-mail, and handing a project over to another owner
  • Signing up yourself or resetting your password by e-mail: an administrator does that

The name

A tick is a check mark, and also the tick of a clock. With a nod to the nursery rhyme “Hickory, dickory, dock”.

How it is built

A sync server and an installable web app in one container, on Node 26 with SQLite. The protocol is described in SPEC.md (in Dutch); the tests run on every change.
