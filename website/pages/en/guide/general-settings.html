General settings

Under Settings and you set a few things that apply to you everywhere: they are stored on the server, so your phone and your computer behave the same.

As in Todoist, a few of these settings have their own section in Settings: what is in the sidebar under , the buttons at the bottom under , snoozing under , and the morning overview and evening review under . They are all described below.

What the app shows when you open it: (the default), or always the Inbox, Today, Upcoming or your notes. If you open the app through a link to a task or a note, that comes first.

Monday (the default), Saturday or Sunday: every week in the calendar starts on that day, in both the month and the week view. Week numbers, your weekly productivity goal and "volgende week" in quick add stay with the week from Monday to Sunday.

Times in the lists, the task window, the calendar, Completed and the notes are shown as 14:30 or as 2:30 PM. You can always type either way: om 14:30 and om 2:30 pm both work.

Normally quick add turns Dentist tomorrow 9:00 into a task for tomorrow at nine. When you switch this off, it simply stays the name of the task, which helps if you often write words like "monday" or "daily" in a task. A deadline in curly braces ({friday}), a reminder with an exclamation mark and the rest (#project, @label, p1) still work. This also applies to tasks you add through the Android app, a widget or the browser extension.

If you do not use notes or labels, for example, you can take them out of the sidebar here. The parts themselves stay: you can still reach them through search or a link.

Which day the date picker uses for Next week and Weekend: Monday and Saturday by default, but you can pick any weekday (a Friday weekend, a Sunday start of the week). Typing next week in quick add follows the same choice; the Dutch volgende week still means the coming Monday.

On a touch screen you can swipe a task to the right or to the left. By default right completes it and left lets you choose a new date. For each direction you can choose None, Select, Complete, Schedule, Reminders or Delete. Completing and scheduling can be undone, and Delete asks first.

Reset subtasks: when you complete a recurring task, its completed subtasks open again for the next time (off by default).

How long a reminder waits when you tap the snooze button in its notification: 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, 1, 6 or 12 hours, or a day (10 minutes by default). The button names the time. It applies to notifications from the server and to the first snooze button of the Android app.

Switch on the Morning overview and the Evening review and choose a time. Every day at that time the Android app tells you how many tasks are due today (in the morning, with how many are overdue) or still left (in the evening; it stays quiet when everything is done). The notification only shows numbers, no task names.

A short tone when you complete a task. Off by default.

On a phone there are three buttons and Browse at the bottom. By default they are Inbox, Today and Upcoming; here you choose three others from Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Notes, Notifications, Completed, Filters and Labels. Browse (with everything from the sidebar) is always to their right.
