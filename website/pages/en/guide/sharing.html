Sharing and working together

Working together happens per project. You share a project with other people on the same server; after that everyone works in the same tasks and sections.

The share window. The owner sees the field to add someone, and next to each member the button to remove that person from the project.

Sharing a project

  1. Open the project and choose . For a project that is already shared, the button is called .
  2. Type the username of the other person and choose . That person needs an account on the same Tickory server. Capitals do not matter.
  3. Choose the role: or .
  4. The person is listed right away, with the role, and sees the project with everything in it, including what was already there. There is no invitation that has to be accepted first.

If the username does not exist, or that account is blocked, you get the message “Couldn't share the project. There's no user with that username.” Inviting by link or by e-mail is not possible; someone without an account first needs one from an administrator.

You recognise a shared project in the sidebar by the sign with two figures. Above the tasks you see who you share it with.

Roles: owner, can edit, view only

Whoever created the project is the owner. Whoever is added gets a role: can edit (the default) or view only. You pick the role in the Share window, next to the username field, and can change it later.

View only is handy for someone who wants to follow along without changing anything: a grandparent who wants to know what has been arranged for the family weekend, or a child who may see the shopping list. Commenting does work, so a question does not have to go outside the app.

An owner cannot leave the project and cannot hand over ownership: the only way out is deleting the project, and then it is gone for everyone.

Changing a role

As the owner: open and choose or next to the name. It applies right away, also in the subprojects you own. When someone becomes a viewer, the tasks assigned to that person are no longer assigned.

When you can only view

In the sidebar the project has an eye instead of the two figures, and above the tasks it says

  • There is no circle to complete a task (it is greyed out), no and no . Dragging and selecting several tasks do not work.
  • You can always open a task: the task window shows everything, with the note that you can only view. Commenting and setting your own reminders work as usual.
  • Sections and subtasks are always expanded: collapsing belongs to the project and applies to everyone, so the app leaves that to those who can edit. You do not choose the view (list, board, calendar) either.
  • In , and filters, dated tasks from such a project show up too, without a circle.
  • In the widgets of the Android app such a task has no circle; tapping that spot opens the task.

Assigning tasks

Open a task in a shared project and pick one of the members under , or . In the list the task then shows “for Bram”; your own tasks show “for you”.

  • The field is only there when the project has at least two members. Viewers are not in the list.
  • Assigning changes nothing about who may see or tick off the task: everyone in the project can.
  • If someone leaves the project or is removed from it, the assignment lapses.
  • If you move a task to a project that person is not a member of, the assignment lapses too.

In and you see all dated tasks from your shared projects, including those assigned to someone else.

Together in the same project

  • What someone else changes shows up for you within a few seconds, without having to refresh the page.
  • You can write comments on a task or on the whole project; everyone in the project sees them and sees who wrote them. With @ you mention someone, who then gets a notification.
  • If someone assigns you a task, comments on your task or someone else completes it, you see that under , and if you like also as a notification on your devices. See Notifications and mentions.
  • If two people change different things about the same task at the same time (one the date, the other the name), both changes are kept. If they change the same field, whoever reached the server last wins.
  • If a change of yours can no longer be applied, for example because someone else deleted the task in the meantime, you get a message and see the actual state again.

Removing someone from the project

As the owner: open and choose next to the name. After confirmation that person no longer sees the project. The tasks stay; only the assignments to that person lapse.

Leaving a project

As a member or viewer: choose and then , or open and choose there. The project disappears from your list; nothing changes for the others. You only get back in when the owner adds you again.

What stays personal

  • Your Inbox. It is yours alone and cannot be shared.
  • Projects you do not share. Nobody else sees them, not even an administrator: an administrator manages accounts, not tasks.
  • Your labels. If you put a label on a task in a shared project, the others see that label on that task, but they cannot rename or delete it (see Labels). They can take it off the task, or put it on another task themselves by typing the same name.
  • Reminders. You set your own reminders, also on tasks in a shared project; nobody else sees or gets them. See Reminders.
  • Filters. These are personal too: nobody else sees your filters. See Filters.

Of the people you share a project with, you only see the name and the username.

Limitations

  • You share a whole project, not a single task or section.
  • There are three fixed roles. A viewer who may complete tasks, or rights per section, do not exist.
  • Handing over ownership is not possible.
  • A comment can only be changed or deleted by whoever wrote it; an owner cannot do that for someone else.
