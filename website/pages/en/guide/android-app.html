What you use now

The web app works in the browser on Android and can be installed as an app there:

  1. Open the address of your Tickory in Chrome and sign in.
  2. In the browser's menu, choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

You get an icon among your apps, a window of its own, and the app also works without a connection. For that the server has to be reachable over HTTPS. More in Install as an app and Working offline.

What the web app on Android does not do

  • Send notifications; see Reminders.
  • Show a widget on your home screen.
  • Receive text from another app through “Share”.
