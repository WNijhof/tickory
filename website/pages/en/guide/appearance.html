Appearance: theme and accent colour

Under Settings and you choose how Tickory looks. A choice takes effect at once and is kept on this device. So your phone can be dark and your computer light.

  • (the default): light or dark, just like the rest of your device. If your device switches to dark in the evening, Tickory follows.
  • or : always that way, whatever your device does.

The colour of buttons, the selected list and today in the calendar. There are eight colours: (the default), , , , , , and . Each colour has a light and a dark variant with enough contrast to stay easy to read.

Good to know

  • The choice is only on this device (in the storage of the browser or the app), not on the server. If you clear the site data or sign out with "clear local data", it goes back to the default.
  • In the demo on this website it only lasts while the page is open.
  • The colours of projects and labels are chosen separately, per project or label.
