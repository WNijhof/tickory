Keyboard shortcuts

The keyboard makes you faster in Tickory. One key, or two in a row, and you are where you want to be.

The overview that opens with ?.

The keys

KeyWhat happens
q: the window to add a task opens. If you are in a project the task goes there; in Today it gets today's date.
g then t.
g then u.
g then i.
?. You can also use the keyboard icon at the bottom of the sidebar.
/ or Ctrl+K: the search window opens, see Search. Ctrl+K also works while you type in a field.
Esc. On a phone it also closes the sidebar.
Ctrl+Enter (on a Mac Cmd+Enter). In the field for a comment it posts the comment.

Good to know

  • For g then t you press the keys one after the other, not together. After the g you have about a second and a half for the second key.
  • So if you type in the input field, a q is just a letter.
  • It does not matter whether the key is a capital. With Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held down the letters do nothing, so the shortcuts of your browser keep working; only Ctrl+K belongs to Tickory.
  • On a phone without a keyboard they are of no use; use the buttons there.
