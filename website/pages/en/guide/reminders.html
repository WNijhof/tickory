Reminders

A reminder is a notification at the moment you need to do something. This part is not finished yet.

In development

In version {{version}} you cannot set reminders in the app and no notification is sent anywhere. This page will be completed once reminders are part of a released version.

What is there now

  • The server can already store reminders and sync them between your devices. The data model knows two kinds: a number of minutes before the time of the task, or a fixed date and time.
  • Reminders are personal: in a shared project everyone will set their own reminders.
  • The app has no screen for them yet, and nothing delivers a notification yet.

What you can do until then

  • Give a task a date with a time, for example morgen om 9 uur (tomorrow at 9). The task is then listed on that day in Vandaag and turns red as soon as the time has passed.
  • Open Vandaag as the fixed start of your day. The number next to it counts what is due today and what is overdue.
  • For something that really must not go wrong, set an alarm or an appointment in your phone's calendar.

What is coming

The plan is that you can set one or more reminders on a task, and that you get a notification for them on your phone or computer. How exactly that will work will be described here once it is finished; until then we promise nothing that does not exist yet. You can follow the state of the work on GitHub.
