The screen: sidebar and task list

The sidebar on the left is for choosing what you see, the task list is on the right. This page explains where everything is and what the circles, numbers and chips mean.

The screen with Today. The sidebar is on the left, the list on the right. Above the list is the input field for adding a task quickly.

Collapsing and expanding subprojects: if a project has subprojects, there is a small arrow in front of it. Click it to hide or show the subprojects. The choice belongs to the project, not to you: it also stays on your other devices, and in a shared project the others see the same.

The app remembers which screen you looked at last on that device.

A row in the list

Every task is one row:

  • The circle on the left is the button to tick the task off. The color of its edge is the priority: red for P1, orange for P2, blue for P3 and grey for P4 (none).
  • The name of the task, and below it in grey the first line of the description, if there is one. Click the name or the row to open the task; see The task window.
  • Chips below that, in this order: the date (its color shows whether it is overdue, today or later; for a recurrence there is a ↻ in front), the deadline, the number of completed subtasks (“1/4”), the number of comments, the labels (@name, in the color of the label), a bell 🔔 if a reminder is going to go off, and who the task is for (“for Bram”, “for you”) in a shared project.
  • The project is on the right, with its color, in Today and Upcoming. If the task is in a section, that is shown too (“Huishouden / Klusjes”).

Subtasks in the list

Subtasks are indented below their task. If a task has subtasks, the “1/4” chip shows a small arrow: click it to hide or show the subtasks. The app remembers that too. You make subtasks in the task window.

Quick buttons

If you point at a row (or reach it with the keyboard), three buttons appear on the right: a pencil to edit the task, a calendar to pick another day and a trash can to delete it. With the calendar button you choose from , , , or . The time and any recurrence stay as they were.

Sections

In a project, tasks stand under the heading of their section, with the number of tasks behind it. Tasks without a section come first.

  • Click the arrow in front of the name to collapse or expand a section. That is kept, just like collapsing and expanding subtasks.
  • The ⋯ to the right of the name opens the section menu: rename and delete.

Ticking off and bringing back

Click the circle to tick a task off. A moment later it disappears from the list and for a few seconds the message appears at the bottom, with the button .

  • Viewing completed tasks: at the bottom of a project is (with the number). Click the circle of a completed task to reopen it. If you reopen a task with subtasks, the subtasks stay ticked until you reopen them yourself.
  • Recurring tasks do not disappear when you tick them off: the date moves on to the next time. See ticking off recurrences.
  • If you tick off a task with subtasks, its subtasks are ticked off too.

Today and Upcoming

  • shows all open tasks dated today, from all your projects, and above them whatever is . If there is nothing to do you see “All done for today”.
  • shows the next fourteen days, starting tomorrow, with a heading per day. Days without tasks are skipped.

Within a day, tasks without a time come first, then those with a time, from early to late; at the same time the highest priority goes first. In a shared project you also see the tasks assigned to someone else here. Tasks without a date are in neither view; you find those in their project.

On a phone

On a narrow screen the sidebar is behind the button with the three lines at the top left. The Share and Project buttons become icons.

On a narrow screen the sidebar is hidden. Tap the button with the three lines at the top left to open it; when you choose something it closes by itself. Tap next to the sidebar (or press Escape) to close it. On a phone the task window takes up the whole screen.

The status in the top right

The top right shows whether everything is up to date (), whether changes are waiting, or whether the server cannot be reached. Click it to sync right away. See Working offline.

What is not possible yet

  • Changing the order. Tasks, sections and projects stay in the order in which they were made; dragging is not possible.
  • A board or calendar view, the duration of a task and attachments.
