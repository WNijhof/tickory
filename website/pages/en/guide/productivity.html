Productivity

How much did you actually get done this week, and are you keeping it up? lays it out: today and this week against your own goals, how many days and weeks in a row you reached them, and where your work went. No points, levels or rewards: just what you did.

Today, this week, your streaks, the last days and weeks, by project and your goals, on one screen.

Opening it

  • Above Completed there is a line with what you completed today and this week and your streak; the button opens the full overview.
  • Or go to (your name at the bottom of the sidebar) and choose .
  • The screen has no line of its own in the sidebar. at the top right takes you back to Completed.

What counts

  • Every completion of yours. Including every time you complete a recurring task (it is not listed in Completed, because it stays open with its next date). A subtask completed together with her task does not count separately.
  • Reopening does not count. If you restore a task (or undo completing it), that completion no longer counts. If you undo completing a recurring task, it does still count, like the task's own counter.
  • Only you. What others complete in a shared project does not count and is not shown here; the projects list only what you completed in them.
  • The projects you can see now, including your Inbox and archived projects. If you leave a project, or it is deleted, the completions in it no longer count.
  • Per day in the time zone of the device you are looking at, like Today and Completed: what you complete at half past midnight belongs to the new day. Daylight saving time makes no difference.
  • 180 days back. That is how long the server keeps what happened (the activity log); before that the overview knows nothing. A streak can therefore be at most that long.

What you see

  • Today and this week: how many you completed, out of how many your goal is, and how many are left (or Goal reached). A week runs from Monday to Sunday.
  • Daily streak: how many days in a row you reached the daily goal, and the longest streak. Today does not break the streak yet: the day is not over. A day off does not break it either.
  • Weekly streak: how many weeks in a row you reached the weekly goal, and the longest.
  • The last 7 days and by week (the last 4 or 12 weeks, chosen with the buttons; this device remembers the choice) as bar charts. The dashed line is your goal; today and this week are the darkest, days off lighter. The week number is below each week. Point at a bar to see the day or week in full with its number. For a screen reader the same numbers are in a table.
  • This week by project: the five projects where you completed the most, in their colour, and the rest together under Other projects.
On a phone everything is stacked.

Goals

At the bottom of the screen you set how many tasks you want to complete: (5 by default) and (25 by default). A goal of 0 turns it off: you then just see the number, without a streak. A goal can be up to 100 a day and 700 a week. The goals belong to your account, so they apply on all your devices, also in the Android app.

Days off

Choose the days on which you do not have to think about your daily goal; Saturday and Sunday by default. A day off does not break your streak. If you reach the daily goal on a day off anyway, that day simply counts. At least one day of the week always counts. The weekly goal ignores days off: everything you complete in the week counts.

Without a connection

The numbers come from the server. The last overview is kept on the device, so without a connection you see what was there, together with when it was updated. When you complete something (here or on another device), the overview updates itself while it is on screen; asks right away. Signing out clears the stored overview.

What it cannot do yet

  • A goal per project, or goals that change over time: a streak always uses your current goal and days off, also for the days before.
  • Looking back further than 180 days, or viewing someone else's overview.
  • A screen of its own in the Android app outside the web app: there you see the same screen as in the browser.
