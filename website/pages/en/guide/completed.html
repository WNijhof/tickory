Completed

What have you actually done these past weeks? in the sidebar lists everything you ticked off, per day, with the newest at the top. A task you ticked off too soon goes back with one click.

Everything you ticked off, per day. Every row has a Restore button.

What you see

  • Per day. The groups are the days on which you ticked tasks off, in the time zone of the device you are looking at. Within a day the latest is at the top. Each task shows its project (with the section) and the time you ticked it off.
  • All projects, or one. Use the list at the top to choose a project (with the number of completed tasks behind it). The project menu also has for that.
  • Search. Type in the search field: titles and descriptions are searched, ignoring capitals and accents (cafe finds Café).
  • Lots of tasks. 100 rows are shown at a time; at the bottom a Show more button gives the next ones. With thousands of completed tasks the screen stays smooth.

Tasks from archived projects are not in it, nor are tasks from a project you are no longer a member of. A subtask that was ticked off together with her task is not listed separately: her task is already there.

Who completed a task and when is in the Activity of the project.

Above the list (for all projects) you see how many tasks you completed today and this week, against your goals, and your daily streak. The button opens the full overview, with the last days and weeks as bar charts: see Productivity.

Restoring a task

  • Click on a task (or on her circle). She is open again in her project, with her old date. A message says so, with in case you change your mind.
  • Several at once: pick them (Selecting several tasks works here too) and use .
  • If you restore a task with subtasks, her subtasks stay completed until you restore them yourself.

Repeating tasks

A repeating task does not disappear when you tick it off: it moves on to its next date and stays open. That is why it is not in ; only the count of how many times it was ticked off goes up. In Productivity every time does count. If you undid ticking off such a task, the old date is back.

What it cannot do yet

  • A list of earlier times you ticked off a repeating task.
  • Choosing completed tasks from a particular period; search by name and look per day.
