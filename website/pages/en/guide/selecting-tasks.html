Selecting several tasks

Move a pile of tasks to tomorrow, bring a whole row to another project or tick off ten tasks at once: pick them, and do it in one go. It works in the list, on the board, in the calendar and in Today, Upcoming, Completed, filters and labels.

Three tasks picked: the bar at the top shows what you can do with them.

Picking tasks

  • With the button (a tick-box icon next to the buttons at the top right; the name is in its tooltip). Every task then gets a box on the left; tick the tasks you want. The circle still completes a task. While you are picking, a click on the row itself picks it too (the task window does not open). stops.
  • Ctrl click (Cmd click on a Mac) on a task picks it or lets it go, without the button. Shift click picks everything from the previously picked task to the one you click, also upwards.
  • With the keyboard: x picks the task under the pointer, or the one with focus if you last moved with Tab. Once you are picking, Space does the same. Ctrl+A picks all tasks in view and Esc stops. In an input field these keys do nothing.
  • On a phone: press and hold a task. It is picked and the bar appears at the bottom; a tap then picks more. If you keep moving after the long press, you drag the task instead of picking it (see Dragging).

The bar shows how many tasks are picked; a screen reader reads that out, and All selected after Ctrl+A. in the bar does the same as that key. Going to another screen clears the picks, and a task that disappears (for example because someone else deletes it) drops out by itself. Tasks that are not in view do not take part: a collapsed section, or a subtask behind a closed arrow, is not picked.

Permissions and what does not work

Every action is simply a series of the same changes as for one task, with the same rules: you can only do what you could do with a single task. Everything goes to the server in one go (and waits patiently if you are offline), and you see the result straight away.

If the server refuses part of it, for example because a colleague deleted a task in the meantime, you get one message: how many of how many did not work, which tasks they were and why. The rest did work.

Dragging and selecting

Dragging and picking work side by side: with the mouse, dragging only starts after a few millimetres of movement, and a click stays a click. If you drag a picked task while more are picked, all picked tasks go along together, in the order of the list, to where you let go (a section, a project in the sidebar, a column on the board, or a day in the calendar). A counter on the dragged row says how many there are. If you drag a task that is not picked, you move only that one.

Dragging together works for up to 100 tasks and only if they all fit in that place. If you let go on one of the picked tasks themselves or a subtask of one, or if there are more than 100, only the task you were holding moves and a message says why; the rest stays picked and goes in one go with .

What it cannot do yet

  • Pick all tasks of a project that are not in view (for example behind a collapsed section): expand first.
  • Set reminders, deadlines or a duration for several tasks at once, or give them a comment together.
  • Keep a selection or take it from one screen to another.
