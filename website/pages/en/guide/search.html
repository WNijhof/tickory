Search

You know you wrote something about the dentist somewhere, but not where? Search for it: in all your tasks, comments, projects, labels and filters at once, while you type.

The search window: results of different kinds, with the words found highlighted.

Opening the search window

  • Press /, or Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac).
  • Or click at the top of the sidebar. On a phone there is a magnifying glass in the bar at the top of the screen.

A / is only a shortcut when you are not typing in a field and no window is open; otherwise it is just a slash. Ctrl+K always works. See also Keyboard shortcuts.

What is searched

KindWhat is searched
The title, the description and the names of the labels. A match in the description or a label shows the piece of text where the word is.
The text of comments on tasks and projects. All words must be in the same comment.
, The name, and for a project the description too.
The name. Also labels that someone else put on a task in a shared project.
The name and the filter text.

Archived projects and sections, and everything in them, are left out. Completed tasks are included, but they come last.

How it searches

  • Capitals and accents do not matter. cafe finds “Café”, zoe finds “Zoë” and ijs finds “IJs kopen”. That also holds when your keyboard delivers an accent as a separate character.
  • All words, in any order. bellen tandarts finds “Tandarts bellen voor controle”.
  • Part of a word is enough. boodschap finds “boodschappen”; the letters found are highlighted.
  • The order of the results. First what starts with your word, then what has it in the title, then what has it only in the description, a label or a comment. Within those groups projects, labels, filters and sections come before tasks, and shorter titles before longer ones.

At most 50 results are shown. A change from another device appears in the list by itself.

Using the keyboard

KeyWhat happens
↓ and ↑Choose the next or previous result; after the last one you are back at the first. Page Up and Page Down jump five results.
EnterOpen the chosen result. The first result is already chosen as soon as there are results.
EscClose the search window.

With the mouse you click a result. Screen readers hear how many results there are and which one is chosen.

What a result opens

  • A task: the task window of that task, on top of the screen you were on.
  • A comment: the task window of the task it belongs to, or for a comment on a project that project with its comments.
  • A project or a section: that project.
  • A label or a filter: the screen with the tasks, see Labels and Filters.
On a phone the search window fills the whole screen.
