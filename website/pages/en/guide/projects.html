Projects and sections

A project is a list of tasks. Projects can have a color, contain subprojects, be a favorite or be archived. Within a project you divide tasks over sections, and you can look at it as a list, board or calendar (see List, board and calendar).

The Inbox

Everyone has their own Inbox: the project where a task ends up if you choose nothing else. The Inbox is always at the top of the sidebar, is yours alone, and cannot be shared, renamed, archived or deleted. You can make sections in it, though.

Making and editing a project

The window for editing a project: name, color and favorite.
  • Making: click the + next to in the sidebar. Give a name, choose a color, tick if you like, and under choose a project this one becomes a subproject of, if needed. Choose ; the new project opens right away.
  • Editing: open the project, choose and then . You change the name, the color and whether it is a favorite. Moving the project into another project is not possible yet.
  • Sharing: see Sharing and working together.

The color, the favorite mark and collapsing or expanding subprojects belong to the project itself, not to you: in a shared project the others see the same color and the same favorites.

Subprojects

A project can hang inside another project. In the project menu choose , or, for a new project, choose the project above it under . In the sidebar a subproject is indented under its project, and an arrow in front of the project collapses or expands the subprojects. In the drop-down lists of the task window they are indented too.

  • A subproject of a shared project is shared along with it.
  • If you delete a project, its subprojects disappear too, with all the sections and tasks in them. Only a subproject that someone else owns stays, at the top level.
  • If you archive a project, its subprojects go along.

Archiving and restoring

A project that is finished, but that you do not want to delete, you archive:

  • Archiving: project menu, (owner only). You go to your Inbox, and for a few seconds a message at the bottom says the project has been archived, with the button . The project (with its subprojects) disappears from the sidebar, the favorites and the drop-down lists.
  • Viewing: at the bottom of the sidebar is , as soon as there is one. The window shows the list; click a name to open the project. An archived project has the message at the top, and you cannot add anything to it.
  • Restoring: choose , in that window or in the bar at the top of the project. The subprojects that were archived along with it come back too.

Tasks and comments in an archived project are kept. Note: tasks with a date from an archived project still appear in and . You cannot archive the Inbox.

Deleting a project

In the project menu choose . After confirmation the project disappears with all the sections, subprojects and tasks in it, also for the people you share it with. That cannot be undone. If you only want it out of sight, archive it instead.

Sections

  • Making: open the project and choose at the bottom.
  • Renaming or deleting: click the ⋯ to the right of the name of the section. If you delete a section, all tasks in it disappear too.
  • Collapsing and expanding: with the arrow in front of the name. See The screen.
  • Putting a task in a section: type /name of the section when adding, choose under the section, or choose the section in the task window.

Tasks without a section are at the top of the project, above the first section.

Labels

A label is a keyword on a task, such as @bellen or @buiten. You set a label with @name when adding, or in the Labels field of the task window. In the list the labels appear under the name of the task.

Choose a label in the sidebar and you see all tasks with it. Renaming, coloring and deleting labels is possible too. See Labels.

Templates

A project you need more than once can be saved as a template with . With the button next to the + under you create a new project from one in a single step, with the dates counted from a start date you choose; six templates also come with the app. See Templates.

The order and subprojects

You drag projects in the sidebar: above or below another project for another place, onto another project to become a subproject of it, and onto the heading to be an ordinary project again. Only the owner can do this. With the keyboard it works with Alt and the arrow keys. See Moving tasks and projects.

What is not possible yet

  • Dragging sections. Sections stay in the order in which they were made. Favourites have no order of their own: they follow the order of the projects.
