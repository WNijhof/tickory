Projects, sections and subtasks

A project is a list of tasks. Inside a project you divide tasks over sections, and a task can have subtasks.

A project with sections. Subtasks are indented under their task; the number in the sidebar is the number of open tasks. (The sample household is Dutch, so the names in it are too.)

The Inbox

Everyone has their own Inbox: the project a task ends up in when you choose nothing else. The Inbox is always at the top, is yours alone, and cannot be shared, renamed or deleted. You can create sections in it.

Projects

  • Creating: click the + next to in the sidebar and give it a name.
  • Renaming: open the project, choose and then .
  • Deleting: choose and then . After confirmation the project disappears with all sections and tasks in it, also for the people you share it with. This cannot be undone.
  • Sharing: see Sharing and working together.

Projects are listed in the sidebar in the order in which they were created. Putting a project inside another project (sub-projects), choosing a colour, marking a project as a favourite or archiving it is not possible in the app yet.

Sections

  • Creating: open the project and choose .
  • Renaming or deleting: click the ⋯ to the right of the section name. If you delete a section, all tasks in it disappear as well.
  • Putting a task in a section: type /name of the section when adding it, or choose the section in the edit window.

Tasks without a section are at the top of the project, above the first section.

Subtasks

Open a task, type in at the bottom and press Enter. That way you quickly add a few in a row. A subtask is an ordinary task itself: click it to give it a date, labels or subtasks of its own.

  • A subtask is always in the same project and section as the task it hangs under. So you choose the project and section on the top-level task.
  • If you tick off a task, its subtasks are ticked off too.
  • If you delete a task, its subtasks are gone too.
  • Turning an existing task into a subtask of another one (or the other way round) is not possible in the app yet.

Ticking off and bringing back

Click the circle in front of a task to tick it off. For a few seconds the message appears at the bottom with the button .

  • Viewing completed tasks: at the bottom of a project you find (with the number of completed tasks). Click the circle of a completed task again to reopen it. If you reopen a task with subtasks, the subtasks stay ticked off until you reopen them yourself.
  • Recurring tasks do not disappear when ticked off: the date moves on to the next occurrence. See ticking off a recurrence.

Deleting a task

Open the task and choose . After confirmation the task is gone, with its subtasks. There is no recycle bin: what you delete, you cannot get back. If you only want a task out of sight, tick it off instead.

Labels

A label is a keyword on a task, such as @bellen or @buiten. You set a label with @name when adding the task, or in the field of the edit window. In the list, labels are shown below the name of the task.

More is not possible yet: there is no screen for renaming or deleting labels, and you cannot filter by a label.

Today and Upcoming

Today: first what is overdue, then what is due today.
  • shows all open tasks dated today, from all your projects, and above them what is . The number in the sidebar counts those two together.
  • shows the next fourteen days, starting tomorrow, with a heading per day. Days without tasks are skipped.

Within a day, tasks without a time come first and then those with a time, from early to late; at the same time the highest priority goes first. Each task shows the project (and section) it is in, and a bell 🔔 if a reminder is going to go off. In a shared project you also see the tasks assigned to someone else here. Tasks without a date are in neither view; you find them in their project.

What is not possible yet

  • Changing the order. Tasks, sections and projects are listed in the order in which they were created; dragging is not possible.
  • Search and filters. The search function and the filter language are built, but there are no screens for them yet, so you cannot use them yet.
  • Comments on a task, attachments, the duration of a task, and a board or calendar view.
