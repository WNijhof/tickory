Projects, sections and subtasks

A project is a list of tasks. Inside a project you divide tasks over sections, and a task can have subtasks.

A project with sections. Subtasks are indented under their task; the number in the sidebar is the number of open tasks.

The Inbox

Everyone has their own Inbox: the project a task ends up in when you choose nothing else. The Inbox is always at the top, is yours alone, and cannot be shared, renamed or deleted. You can create sections in it.

Projects

  • Creating: click the + next to Projecten in the sidebar and give it a name.
  • Renaming: open the project, choose Project… and then Naam wijzigen (change name).
  • Deleting: choose Project… and then Project verwijderen (delete project). After confirmation the project disappears with all sections and tasks in it, also for the people you share it with. This cannot be undone.
  • Sharing: see Sharing and working together.

Projects are listed in the sidebar in the order in which they were created. Putting a project inside another project (sub-projects), choosing a colour, marking a project as a favourite or archiving it is not possible in the app yet.

Sections

  • Creating: open the project and choose Sectie toevoegen (add section).
  • Renaming or deleting: click the ⋯ to the right of the section name. If you delete a section, all tasks in it disappear as well.
  • Putting a task in a section: type /name of the section when adding it, or choose the section in the edit window.

Tasks without a section are at the top of the project, above the first section.

Editing a task

Click the name of a task. In the window that opens, you change:

The edit window. The field Toegewezen aan (assigned to) is only there in a project you share with others.
Name and description (Beschrijving)
A task with a description is marked in the list with the word “notitie” (note).
Date (Datum)
In plain Dutch, including recurrences. See Dates and recurrences.
Deadline
The final day, separate from the date on which you want to work on it.
Priority (Prioriteit)
P1 (highest) to P4 (none). You can tell the priority from the colour of the circle: red, orange, blue or grey.
Assigned to (Toegewezen aan)
Only in a shared project. See assigning tasks.
Labels
Names separated by commas, for example bellen, thuis. A new label is created.
Project and section (Sectie)
Choose another project or section to move the task; subtasks move along. The task goes to the bottom there.

Choose Opslaan (save) to keep the changes, or Annuleren (cancel, or Escape) to discard them.

Subtasks

Open a task, type in Subtaak toevoegen (add subtask) at the bottom and press Enter. That way you quickly add a few in a row. A subtask is an ordinary task itself: click it to give it a date, labels or subtasks of its own.

  • A subtask is always in the same project and section as the task it hangs under. So you choose the project and section on the top-level task.
  • If you tick off a task, its subtasks are ticked off too.
  • If you delete a task, its subtasks are gone too.
  • Turning an existing task into a subtask of another one (or the other way round) is not possible in the app yet.

Ticking off and bringing back

Click the circle in front of a task to tick it off. For a few seconds the message “Taak voltooid” (task completed) appears at the bottom with the button Ongedaan maken (undo).

  • Viewing completed tasks: at the bottom of a project you find Voltooide taken tonen (show completed tasks) with their number. Click the circle of a completed task again to reopen it. If you reopen a task with subtasks, the subtasks stay ticked off until you reopen them yourself.
  • Recurring tasks do not disappear when ticked off: the date moves on to the next occurrence. See ticking off a recurrence.

Deleting a task

Open the task and choose Verwijderen (delete). After confirmation the task is gone, with its subtasks. There is no recycle bin: what you delete, you cannot get back. If you only want a task out of sight, tick it off instead.

Labels

A label is a keyword on a task, such as @bellen or @buiten. You set a label with @name when adding the task, or in the Labels field of the edit window. In the list, labels are shown below the name of the task.

More is not possible yet: there is no screen for renaming or deleting labels, and you cannot filter by a label.

Today and Upcoming

Vandaag: first what is overdue, then what is due today.
  • Vandaag (today) shows all open tasks dated today, from all your projects, and above them what is te laat (overdue). The number in the sidebar counts those two together.
  • Binnenkort (upcoming) shows the next fourteen days, starting tomorrow, with a heading per day. Days without tasks are skipped.

Within a day, tasks without a time come first and then those with a time, from early to late; at the same time the highest priority goes first. Each task shows the project (and section) it is in. In a shared project you also see the tasks assigned to someone else here. Tasks without a date are in neither view; you find them in their project.

What is not possible yet

  • Changing the order. Tasks, sections and projects are listed in the order in which they were created; dragging is not possible.
  • Searching your tasks.
  • Comments on a task, attachments, the duration of a task, and a board or calendar view.
