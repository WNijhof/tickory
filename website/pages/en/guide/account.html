Account, password and devices

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the settings, and choose Account, wachtwoord en apparaten (account, password and devices).

The account window. The button Gebruikersbeheer (user management) is only there for administrators.

Settings

Behind your name at the bottom of the sidebar there are three choices:

  • Account, wachtwoord en apparaten: the window on this page.
  • Nu bijwerken (sync now): fetches the latest state from the server right away and sends what was still waiting.
  • Afmelden en lokale gegevens wissen (sign out and erase local data): see signing out.

Signed-in devices

Every browser and every device you sign in on gets its own session. The list shows a name for each session, such as “Chrome op Android”, and when it was last used. The session you are working with now is marked dit apparaat (this device).

  • Choose Afmelden (sign out) next to another device to sign it out immediately, for example when you have lost a phone. That device then has to sign in again.
  • If you do not recognise a device, sign it out and change your password.
  • The name is determined when signing in and cannot be changed. The same name twice means you signed in twice with the same kind of browser.

Changing your password

  1. Open Wachtwoord wijzigen (change password).
  2. Enter your current password and the new one twice.
  3. Choose Wachtwoord wijzigen.

A password has at least 10 characters and may not be the same as your username. After changing it you stay signed in on this device; all your other devices are signed out and have to sign in again with the new password.

If you no longer know your current password, ask an administrator for a new one; see forgotten password.

Your name and username

The top of the window shows your name and your username. The name is what others see in a shared project; the username is what you sign in with and what others use to share a project with you. Neither can be changed in the app.

For administrators

If you are an administrator, it says so (“beheerder”) after your username and the window has a button Gebruikersbeheer. See Managing users.
