Signed-in devices

Every browser and every device you sign in on gets its own session. The list shows a name for each session, such as “Chrome on Android”, and when it was last used. The session you are working with now is marked .

  • Choose next to another device to sign it out immediately, for example when you have lost a phone. That device then has to sign in again.
  • If you do not recognise a device, sign it out and change your password.
  • The name is determined when signing in and cannot be changed. The same name twice means you signed in twice with the same kind of browser.

Changing your password

  1. Open .
  2. Enter your current password and the new one twice.
  3. Choose .

A password has at least 10 characters and may not be the same as your username. After changing it you stay signed in on this device; all your other devices are signed out and have to sign in again with the new password.

If you no longer know your current password, ask an administrator for a new one; see forgotten password.

The language of the app

The app is available in English and in Dutch. As long as you choose nothing, it follows your browser: if one of your browser's languages is Dutch, the app is Dutch, otherwise English. To decide for yourself, choose and then English or Nederlands; those two names are the same in every language, so you can always find the choice.

  • The switch is immediate, without reloading the page. The choice applies to this device and this browser, and is kept even after signing out; it is not a setting of your account.
  • Notifications arrive in the language you chose on that device.
  • What you type is a different matter: dates and recurrences (morgen om 9 uur) are recognised in Dutch only for now, also in the English interface. It gives a hint if you type an English date. A date such as 31-12, 31-12-2026 or 2026-12-31T14:30 works in any language.
  • The names of projects, tasks and labels are whatever you type; the app does not translate them.

Your name and username

The top of the window shows your name and your username. The name is what others see in a shared project; the username is what you sign in with and what others use to share a project with you. Neither can be changed in the app.
