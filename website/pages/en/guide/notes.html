Notes

Not everything is a task. A shopping list, a recipe, an idea or a bike route goes in : notes with formatting, a colour or pattern, labels and a checklist you tick off as you go. Your notes are only for you and are on all your devices, also without a connection.

Writing with formatting

Formatting shows straight away: bold is bold, a list is a list. There are three ways to format.

  • The toolbar above the text (on a phone at the bottom, above the keyboard): the text style (normal or a heading), , , , , in five colours, code, a link, the lists, a quote, a code block, indenting and undo.
  • A slash. Type / at the start of a line (or after a space) and pick from the menu: , a heading, a list, a quote, code, a table, a divider or a link. Keep typing to search (/head shows the headings).
  • Characters at the start of a line, as in many other apps: - and a space for a list, 1. for a numbered list, [] for a checklist, #, ## or ### for a heading, > for a quote, ``` for code and --- for a line.

With the keyboard: Ctrl+B, I and U for bold, italic and underline, Ctrl+K for a link, Tab and Shift+Tab to indent or outdent an item, Ctrl+Z to undo. On a Mac it is Cmd instead of Ctrl. To open a link in the text: Ctrl+click, or put the cursor in it and choose the link button.

When you paste text from a website or from another note, the formatting (headings, lists, bold, links) is kept. Anything else a web page sends along, such as scripts or images, is left out.

Pictures and audio

  • The paper clip in the toolbar (or in the / menu) adds pictures and audio. On a phone you can take a photo straight away.
  • Paste a screenshot right into the note with Ctrl+V. You can also drag files onto the note.
  • The microphone records audio (ten minutes at most). The first time, your browser or phone asks whether that's allowed.
  • Tap a picture to view it large, save it or delete it; ▶ plays audio. The card in the list shows the pictures.
  • A note can hold twenty pictures and audio clips. Other files (like a PDF) go on a task.

Earlier versions

Tickory keeps an earlier version of your note as soon as you write again after a break (or change the note on another device). The menu has : pick a version to read it, or choose to see what changed after it. brings the old text back; your current text is kept as a version, and puts it right back. The last fifty versions of each note are kept.

Importing from Google Keep or Markdown

  • Google Keep: at Google Takeout, request only "Keep" and download the zip. In your notes, choose and then , and pick that zip (or single .json files). Titles, text, checklists, labels, colours, pinning and pictures come along; archived notes can go to the archive. What's in the Keep bin is left out.
  • Markdown: pick .md or .txt files, or a folder as a zip (for example from Obsidian). The title comes from a first line with # or from the file name; tags in the header become labels; pictures inside the zip become pictures on the note.
  • You can also drag the files onto your notes overview. Before anything happens, you see what was found and what is skipped.
