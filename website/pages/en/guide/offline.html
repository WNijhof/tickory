Working offline

The app keeps a copy of your tasks on your device. Without a connection you simply carry on in that copy; as soon as the server is reachable again, everything syncs.

What you need

  • You have opened the app on this device at least once with a connection, and signed in.
  • The app is reachable over HTTPS. Browsers only allow an app to be stored for offline use on a secure address (the only exception is localhost). Without HTTPS the app works as long as there is a connection, but it does not open without one. For administrators: see HTTPS.

What you can do offline

Everything to do with your tasks: adding, editing, ticking off, moving, creating projects and sections. You see the result immediately; the change waits on your device until it can go to the server.

What does need a connection: signing in, changing your password, the list of devices and user management. If you share a project while offline, that is only carried out once there is a connection again, and only then do you see the other person in the list.

Click the status to sync right away. You can also do that through Instellingen (your name at the bottom of the sidebar) and then Nu bijwerken (sync now).

When the connection comes back

  • The app retries by itself, first after a few seconds and then with longer and longer pauses, up to a minute at most. It also syncs immediately when you bring the app to the front again or your device comes back online.
  • Your waiting changes go to the server in the order in which you made them. Each change is carried out at most once, even if the connection drops halfway.
  • What others changed in the meantime comes in. Your waiting changes are kept while that happens.
  • If you and someone else changed the same field of the same task, whoever reached the server last wins.
  • If a change can no longer be applied, for example because the task was deleted in the meantime, you get a message about it (“Niet gelukt …”, failed) and the app shows the actual state again.

Several devices and tabs

You can be signed in on several devices at once. Each device has its own copy and its own queue. A change on one device shows up on the other within a few seconds, if both have a connection. Two tabs in the same browser share one copy.

Things to watch out for

  • Signing out erases the copy on that device, including changes that were still waiting. The app warns you if that is the case. So sync first before you sign out.
  • The browser manages the storage. If you clear the site's data in your browser, or use a private window, the copy is gone; everything that was already on the server comes back the next time you open the app.
  • If the browser cannot store anything at all, the app says so: “Lokale opslag is niet beschikbaar” (local storage is not available). The app then works, but only while the window is open and there is a connection.
  • A new version of the app is fetched in the background and is active when you open the app again after that.
