Activity

Who ticked off the groceries, and when was that new task added? Every project keeps track of who did what. You find it under in the menu of the project.

Opening

  1. Open the project and choose .
  2. Choose . With you return to the tasks.

It works in every project, also one you share with nobody, and also when you can only view a shared project.

What you see

  • Per day, the newest on top, with the time.
  • Who did what: a task added, changed (with what changed: the title, the date, the priority, an assignment), completed or reopened, moved (to another section or another project), deleted or restored; a section added, renamed or deleted; a comment written or deleted; an attachment added or deleted; someone added, a role changed, someone removed from the project or leaving it; the project created, renamed or archived.
  • The task: below the line is its title. Click it to open the task. If the task is no longer in this project (deleted or moved), it says .
  • More: 50 entries at a time; brings the next ones.
  • Filtering: choose at the top what you want to see: , , , , , or .

If someone else does something while you are looking, it appears by itself after a few seconds. fetches it right away.

What is not in it

  • The text of comments and descriptions: you see that someone commented or changed the description; the text itself is with the task.
  • What is personal: reminders, places, labels (only that the labels of a task changed), filters and templates.
  • Collapsing and the order of tasks.
  • The name of an attachment that was deleted.

Who sees it

  • Everyone who is in the project now, also those who can only view. Whoever joins later also sees what happened before.
  • Whoever leaves the project or is removed from it no longer sees it.
  • Names appear as everywhere in the app; someone who no longer shares a project with you is called .
  • If you delete a project, its activity is gone too.

Without a connection

The activity is stored on the server, not on your device. Without a connection you see what you had already loaded in this session, with the message If you had loaded nothing yet, it says

Limitations

  • The server keeps the activity for 180 days, and per project the last 10,000 entries.
  • There is one list per project; searching the activity, or seeing all projects together, is not possible.
  • What happened before this version of Tickory is not in it.
