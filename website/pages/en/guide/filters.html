Filters

A filter is a saved search on your tasks, for example “everything due today or overdue, without subtasks”. You write it in a short language of its own, and afterwards it is in the sidebar, with the number of tasks next to it.

A filter with its tasks. In the sidebar it is under Filters, with the number of tasks.

Making a filter

The window to make a filter, with the result right away.
  1. Click the + next to in the sidebar, or choose on the Filters screen.
  2. Give the filter a name and type the filter text. Right below it says whether it works and how many tasks it gives now.
  3. Optionally pick a color, and tick to put the filter at the top of the list.
  4. Choose . The filter opens right away.

A filter with an error is not saved: the window first points out what is wrong. Under are the explanation and all examples; clicking an example puts it in the field at the cursor.

Dutch and English words always both work, also mixed: vandaag & p1 and today & p1 give the same. Capitals and accents do not matter, not even in the names of projects, sections and labels. A filter only shows open tasks, unless completed is in it; tasks in an archived project are left out.

Examples

The examples are checked against the sample data of the demo: the project Huishouden, the label bellen and a label of someone else. The sample data is Dutch.

Several lists in one filter

A comma makes separate lists, each with a name of its own. The screen shows them below each other with their name as a heading. A task that fits in more than one list is shown in every list.

Home: #Huishouden, Texel: #"Vakantie Texel"Two lists, Home and Texel. A name with a space goes between quotation marks.

When something is wrong

An error in the filter is pointed out, in the language of your app.

The message is in the language of the app and points at the spot. These are the messages you can get:

You typeMessage
today & #KitchenUnknown project: “Kitchen”. The same for a section or a label that does not exist.
today tomorrowPut & (and) or | (or) here.
today &A term is missing here.
(today | tomorrowThis parenthesis is never closed.
soonUnknown term: “soon”.
31-02Not a valid date: “31-02”.

If a project, section or label that a saved filter refers to disappears or changes later, the filter no longer works. It then has an exclamation mark in the sidebar, and the filter’s screen shows the error with its spot, a button and a button. The filter stays saved as you typed it; change it and it works again.

Editing, favorites and deleting

  • Editing: open the filter and choose . You can change the name, text, color and favorite.
  • Favorite: choose and then , or click the star on the screen. Favorites are at the top of the list, with a star.
  • Deleting: and then , or the bin on the screen. Your tasks stay.
  • Overview: click in the sidebar (the heading itself). The arrow next to it folds the list in or out.

Filters are personal: even in a shared project nobody else sees your filters.

What is not possible yet

  • Dragging filters or giving them an order of your own: they are in the order in which they were made, favorites first.
  • Dates in a filter: the fixed forms (31-12, dec 31, morgen, tomorrow) work in both languages; other plain language, like volgende week donderdag, is only recognized in Dutch.
