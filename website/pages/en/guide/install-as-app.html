Install as an app

Tickory is a web app that you install as an app from your browser. It then gets its own icon and its own window, and opens without a connection too.

Requirement

Installing is only possible when your Tickory is reachable over HTTPS (an address that starts with https://). If your browser does not offer to install it, that is usually the reason. For administrators: see HTTPS.

On a phone, the menu is behind the button with the three lines in the top left.

On an Android phone

  1. Open the address of your Tickory in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Open the browser's menu (the three dots) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. Tickory is now among your apps.

Other browsers on Android, such as Edge, Firefox and Samsung Internet, have a similar option in their menu. There will also be an Android app (APK) of its own, with reminders as alarms on the phone; the first one comes with the next version.

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the address of your Tickory in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap the share button (the square with the arrow pointing up) and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

On a computer

  • Chrome and Edge (Windows, macOS, Linux): open Tickory and click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or choose the option to install the site as an app in the browser's menu.
  • Safari on a Mac: choose Add to Dock in the File menu.
  • Firefox on a computer cannot install web apps. Use Tickory in an ordinary tab there; it works the same otherwise, offline too.

The exact names in the menus differ per browser and per version.

What you get, and what you do not

  • Its own icon and a window without an address bar.
  • The app also opens without a connection and keeps what you do then; see Working offline.
  • You stay signed in until you sign out yourself. On an iPhone or iPad you sign in once more in the installed app.
  • Notifications for reminders can be turned on in the installed app (, over HTTPS). On an iPhone or iPad they only work in the app from the home screen. See Reminders.
  • No widgets and no shortcuts to a particular project.

Removing the app again

Sign out in the app first (, then ), so that the copy of your tasks is off the device. Then remove the app the way you remove any app. Everything stays on the server.
