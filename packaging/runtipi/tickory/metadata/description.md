# Tickory

Tickory is a to-do list and notes app you host yourself: for you, your family or a small team. A free Todoist alternative with no paid tier.

- **Tasks:** projects, sections, subtasks, labels, priorities, deadlines, durations, comments and attachments.
- **Quick add in plain language:** `Call the dentist tomorrow at 9am #Home @calls p1 with a reminder 30 minutes before`, recurrences like "every mon, wed and fri".
- **Views:** Today, Upcoming, list, board and calendar, search, filters, templates and a productivity overview.
- **Together:** shared projects (edit or view only), assigning, @-mentions and notifications.
- **Notes:** formatting, checklists, colours, labels, pictures and audio, version history and sharing.
- **Apps:** offline web app (PWA), Android app with widgets, Windows desktop app and a browser extension.
- **Accounts:** multiple users, two-step verification, SSO (OpenID Connect) and backups; import from Todoist and Google Keep.

The admin account from the form is created at the first start; you create the other accounts in the app.
